# The Monkey's Paw — research notes (fixture)

Public-domain short story by W. W. Jacobs, first published in 1902 (collected in *The Lady of the Barge*). Adapted freely per spec 9.1.

- On a cold, wet night the Whites (Mr and Mrs White and their son Herbert) are visited by Sergeant-Major Morris, back from India. [S1]
- Morris produces a mummified monkey's paw, on which a fakir put a spell so that three separate men could each have three wishes. [S1]
- Morris throws the paw on the fire; Mr White snatches it out. [S1]
- Mr White later wishes for two hundred pounds. [S1]
